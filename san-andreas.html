The Story

A homecoming that turns into a fight to take back a neighborhood, a family, and a state.

01

A funeral brings him home

Carl "CJ" Johnson left Los Santos years ago to build a life elsewhere. News of his mother's death pulls him back to Grove Street, where the family he grew up with has scattered and the block he remembers is falling apart.

02

Dirty cops, deeper trouble

Before he can even grieve, CJ is shaken down by corrupt police officers who frame him for a crime he didn't commit, forcing him into a string of jobs that keep dragging him further from a clean exit.

03

The Families are falling apart

The Grove Street Families, once the toughest crew in Los Santos, are outgunned and outnumbered by rival gangs. Old friendships strain under suspicion, and CJ has to decide who he can actually trust.

04

Chasing leads out of the city

The search for answers about his mother's death and his brother's choices sends CJ north to San Fierro and out to the desert glitz of Las Venturas, working with smugglers, gamblers, and old acquaintances along the way.

05

Coming back for the block

Every road eventually leads back to Grove Street. What started as one man trying to bury his mother in peace turns into a reckoning with everyone who let the neighborhood rot — including some of his own.

The State

Three cities, three moods, one long stretch of highway between them.

South · gang territory

Los Santos

Sun-bleached blocks, low-riders, and a turf war that never really ended. This is where CJ's story starts and where it keeps pulling him back.

North · fog and industry

San Fierro

Steep hills, dockyards, and garages full of cars waiting to be stripped or souped up. A quieter city, but with its own criminal machinery underneath.

Desert · neon and money

Las Venturas

Casinos, showgirls, and fortunes won and lost overnight. Everyone here is working an angle, and CJ has to learn the games fast.

The Cast

The people CJ can lean on, and the ones he has to watch.

Protagonist

Carl "CJ" Johnson

A man trying to do right by his family in a city that keeps handing him wrong choices. Sharp, loyal, and increasingly worn down by everyone asking favors.

Older brother

Sean "Sweet" Johnson

Grove Street's last true believer. Sweet wants the old neighborhood back exactly as it was, even when nobody around him still believes that's possible.

Childhood friend

Melvin "Big Smoke" Harris

Easygoing, always hungry, always around — until the choices he's been quietly making start to look like the biggest problem CJ has to solve.

Corrupt officer

Officer Frank Tenpenny

A cop who treats the whole city as his personal shakedown. He owns CJ's freedom on paper, and he's not shy about reminding him of it.

Cousin

Catalina

A whirlwind CJ meets early on the road — impulsive, dangerous, and impossible to plan around. Working with her rarely goes the way he expects.

Mentor figure

The Truth

An aging conspiracy-minded grower out in the countryside, good for a job and an opinion on absolutely everything going on in the state.

How It Unfolds

The shape of CJ's journey, act by act.

ACT I

Grove Street

CJ reconnects with what's left of the Families and starts rebuilding a crew from almost nothing, one small job and one recovered block at a time.

ACT II

Countryside & San Fierro

Forced out of the city, CJ picks up new skills and new allies in the hills and in San Fierro's garages, slowly building the resources for a real comeback.

ACT III

Las Venturas

The stakes go up in the desert, where CJ gets tangled in casino heists, mob politics, and a shot at real money — and real leverage.

ACT IV

Return to Los Santos

With the pieces finally in place, CJ turns back toward home to settle old scores and take back the neighborhood, block by block.

The Feel of It

What makes this world instantly recognizable.

The radio

Every drive across the state comes with a soundtrack — West Coast rap, funk, and talk radio hosts arguing about the news of the day.

The lowriders

Hydraulics, custom paint, and a competitive scene built entirely around who can make their car bounce the highest.

The turf

Every block has a color, and every color has a history. Territory isn't just geography — it's identity.
